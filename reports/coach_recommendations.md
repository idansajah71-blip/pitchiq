# Recommendations for coaches

Derived from PitchIQ xG (non-penalty shots). Numbers are descriptive averages from the dataset, not guarantees.

1. Aston Villa take 55% of their shots below 0.05 xG (their PitchIQ xG/shot 0.073 vs league 0.098). If just one in three of those hopeless attempts were replaced with a medium-quality (~0.10 xG) look, that alone adds about 5.7 xG per season. Instruction: fewer low-probability shots, more ball progression into the box.
2. Stoke City shoot from 30+ units out for 14% of their attempts (59 shots: xG/shot 0.016, conversion 0.0%). Their xG difference for vs against is -13.11. Instruction: keep long shots as a rebound option only, not a first choice.
3. 'From Counter' shots average 0.170 xG (conversion 14.7%) vs 0.104 for regular play. Increase tempo and shots out of this pattern: 558 shots now, each extra 20 shots in this pattern is approximately +3.40 xG.
4. Inside the attacking bands, the band 110+ / 20-40 is the highest-value zone (mean xG 0.184, conversion 18.3%) but only takes 14.1% of all shots. Drill a higher share of entries into that corridor before shooting.
5. Lionel Andrés Messi Cuccittini has 30 goals on 22.60 xG (217 shots, +7.40 vs expectation, 0.104 xG/shot). Design volume for this finisher; if shots can be steered to him, expected output rises.
