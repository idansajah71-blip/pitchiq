# Recommendations for coaches

Derived from PitchIQ xG (non-penalty shots). Numbers are descriptive averages from the dataset, not guarantees.

1. Aston Villa take 54% of their shots below 0.05 xG (PitchIQ xG/shot 0.073 vs dataset mean 0.098). Scenario, not a causal forecast: replacing one-third of these 212 shots (mean current xG 0.027) with chances rated 0.10 xG would add about +5.2 expected goals if other factors stayed constant. Coaching hypothesis: look for better progression into the box rather than treating low xG alone as proof a shot was a mistake.
2. Stoke City attempt shots from 30+ StatsBomb pitch-coordinate units on 14% of their attempts (59 shots: xG/shot 0.017, conversion 0.0%). Their xG difference for vs against is -13.97. Coaching hypothesis: review whether progression or a pass creates a better option in these situations; low xG alone does not prove a shot was a mistake.
3. 'From Counter' shots average 0.146 xG (conversion 14.7%) vs 0.097 for regular play, across 558 observed shots. This association is descriptive—not proof that the pattern or tempo causes better chances; shot location and game state may explain part of the gap. Review the transition clips before prescribing a tactical change.
4. The attacking band 110+ / inside channel (mirrored y-bands grouped together) has the highest mean xG among qualified paired zones: 0.178 (conversion 17.7%; 3317 shots, 27.5% of all shots). Treat this as a location pattern to review on video, not proof that forcing shots into that band will improve outcomes.
5. Lionel Andrés Messi Cuccittini (Barcelona, La Liga) recorded 27 goals on 19.94 PitchIQ xG (190 shots, +7.06 goals minus xG, 0.105 xG/shot). This is an observed residual, not proof it will persist; validate on a larger or later sample before treating it as repeatable finishing skill.
